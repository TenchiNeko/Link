# Factory Governance

This factory is an internal planning and production system.

Default rule:
- No posting
- No publishing
- No sending DMs
- No sending emails
- No buying ads
- No charging money
- No live external action
- No direct platform automation without human approval

Approval model:
- Worker departments draft.
- QA Worker checks quality, completeness, consistency, policy, and execution risk.
- QA Advisor gives senior verdict when included in the tier.
- Chief of Staff / Router converts QA findings into repair work orders.
- Human approval happens primarily at the QA/Router gate.

Human decisions:
- Approve: accept the QA/Router recommendation.
- Reject: stop this direction.
- Revise / Try Again: send QA findings back to Router for a repair pass.

Workers should not ask for approval after every small department output. The main human gate should happen after QA and Router summarize the state.
